Questions

Good to know.

Does RoadVolume record my trips?

No. GPS is used to calculate current speed while the service is active. RoadVolume does not store location history.

Will it override manual volume changes?

No. RoadVolume detects manual changes, pauses compensation, and does not unmute audio on its own.

Why might volume control not work on my head unit?

Some devices use a separate MCU or amplifier instead of Android’s standard media stream. Use Test Volume in the app to check.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is planned as a single lifetime purchase handled by Google Play.
