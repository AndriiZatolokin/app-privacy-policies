RoadVolume Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-25

RoadVolume adjusts Android media volume based on vehicle speed or GPS speed. The app is designed to work locally on the device.

Data RoadVolume Uses

RoadVolume may access:

  • Location data, to calculate GPS speed when vehicle speed is not available.
  • Audio volume state, to read and adjust the selected Android audio stream.

RoadVolume does not require an account, does not include ads, does not use analytics, and does not request internet access.

Background Location

If the user enables automatic startup and GPS speed, RoadVolume may access location data when the app is closed or not in use. This is used only to start GPS speed monitoring after the car or head unit boots and to adjust volume based on speed.

Location data is processed on the device. RoadVolume does not sell location data and does not share location data for advertising.

Data Sharing

RoadVolume does not transmit personal data to the developer or to third parties.

Data Retention

RoadVolume stores app settings locally on the device, such as volume curve, selected speed source, and startup preference. It does not store location history.

User Controls

Users can disable RoadVolume at any time from the app. Users can also revoke location, notification, or background location permissions in Android system settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address listed on the Google Play Store listing.
