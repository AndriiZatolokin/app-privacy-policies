Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

RoadVolume adjusts Android audio volume based on vehicle speed or GPS speed. The app is designed to perform its core work locally on the device.

Data RoadVolume uses

RoadVolume may access:

  • Location data to calculate GPS speed.
  • Vehicle speed provided by Android Automotive, when available.
  • Audio volume state to read and adjust the selected Android audio stream.
  • Local purchase state to remember whether lifetime Pro access is unlocked.

RoadVolume does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not use analytics.

Location and background use

RoadVolume uses location to calculate current GPS speed for automatic volume adjustment. Location is processed on the device, and RoadVolume does not store location history.

If the user enables automatic startup and selects GPS or automatic speed mode, RoadVolume may use background location after the vehicle or head unit boots to start speed monitoring. This access is used only for the enabled volume-adjustment function.

RoadVolume does not sell location data or use it for advertising.

Purchases and network access

RoadVolume uses Google Play Billing for the optional lifetime Pro purchase. Google Play processes the transaction and may handle purchase and account information under Google’s own privacy terms. RoadVolume stores only the local access state needed to make unlocked features available.

Internet and network-state permissions are included for Google Play Billing. RoadVolume does not send location history or analytics to the developer.

Data sharing

RoadVolume does not transmit location or audio-volume data to the developer. It does not share data for advertising or analytics. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play as described above.

Data retention

RoadVolume stores app settings locally on the device, including the volume curve, selected speed source, selected audio output, startup preference, and unlock state. It does not store location history.

User controls

Users can disable RoadVolume at any time. Location, background location, and notification permissions can be revoked in Android system settings. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data; Google Play may retain the purchase record so an eligible purchase can be restored.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RoadVolume’s functionality or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email address listed on the RoadVolume Google Play page.
