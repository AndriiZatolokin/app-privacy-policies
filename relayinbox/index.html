Your SMS, in your Telegram chat.

A personal Android relay with a local delivery queue, optional on-device translation, and call state and battery alerts. Configure your own bot and control forwarding from your phone.

SMS forwarding sends data to Telegram. Translation runs locally, while Google SDKs can collect technical diagnostics. Read the policy before using the app.
